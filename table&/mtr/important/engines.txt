===========================================
TABLAS NASA PARA DISEÑO DE MOTOR LOX/CH4
===========================================

-------------------------------------------
1. CAMARA DE COMBUSTION (NASA SP-8120)
-------------------------------------------
Pc = 150–300 bar
Tc = 3300–3800 K
O/F = 2.7–3.8
c* = 1500–1900 m/s
CF = 1.4–1.8
Heat flux q'' = 5–20 MW/m2
Conductividad liner = 20–120 W/mK

Ecuaciones:
At = (m_dot * c*) / Pc
Dt = 2 * sqrt(At / π)
L_c = 1.5 * Dt
V_c = π * (Dt/2)^2 * L_c

-------------------------------------------
2. TOBERA (NASA THRUST CHAMBER DESIGN)
-------------------------------------------
Ae/At = 20–40
Ángulo divergencia = 12–18 grados
Longitud tobera L = 0.8–1.2 * Dt
Presión salida Pe = 0.1–1 bar
Heat flux = 2–8 MW/m2

Perfil Rao:
r(z) = r_t + (r_e - r_t)(10t^3 - 15t^4 + 6t^5)

Volumen:
V_n = ∫ π r(z)^2 dz

-------------------------------------------
3. INYECTOR (NASA INJECTOR DESIGN)
-------------------------------------------
Diámetro orificio = 0.5–2.0 mm
Número orificios = 100–3000
ΔP inyector = 0.1–0.3 Pc
Ángulo spray = 20–60 grados

m_dot = Cd * A * sqrt(2 * rho * ΔP)

-------------------------------------------
4. TURBOBOMBAS (NASA TURBOPUMP MANUAL)
-------------------------------------------
RPM LOX = 30k–36k
RPM CH4 = 25k–32k
Presión salida = 250–350 bar
Potencia eje = 5–20 MW
NPSH = 3–10 m
Temperatura turbina = 900–1200 K

P = (m_dot * ΔP) / η

-------------------------------------------
5. PREBURNERS FFSC (NASA SP-8120)
-------------------------------------------
Presión preburner = 250–300 bar
Temperatura gas = 900–1200 K
Riqueza OX-rich = 20–40%
Riqueza Fuel-rich = 10–30%
Caudal gas turbina = 5–20 kg/s
Velocidad gas = 300–600 m/s

-------------------------------------------
6. MATERIALES UHTC (NASA TM)
-------------------------------------------
ZrB2 conductividad = 60–120 W/mK
HfC conductividad = 20–40 W/mK
Temp máx ZrB2 = 3300 K
Temp máx HfC = 3900 K

-------------------------------------------
7. CUASICRISTALES (NASA TM)
-------------------------------------------
Al-Cu-Fe conductividad = 2–10 W/mK
Ti-Zr-Ni conductividad = 1–5 W/mK
Temp máx = 1100–1400 K

-------------------------------------------
8. GEOMETRIA AEROSPIKE (NASA XRS-2200)
-------------------------------------------
Voxel size = 0.1–1.0 mm

Perfil aerospike:
r(z) = r0 + k * z^n

Volumen aerospike:
V = ∫ π r(z)^2 dz

Expansión aerospike:
Ae/At = función(z, presión ambiente)

Heat flux aerospike:
q'' = 3–12 MW/m2

Materiales aerospike:
ZrB2 + HfC + cuasicristales

\section*{NASA Rocket Engine Design Tables}

\subsection*{1. Combustion Chamber}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
$P_c$ & 150--300 bar \\
$T_c$ & 3300--3800 K \\
$O/F$ & 2.7--3.8 \\
$c^*$ & 1500--1900 m/s \\
$q''$ & 5--20 MW/m$^2$ \\
\end{tabular}



\[
A_t = \frac{\dot{m} c^*}{P_c}
\]




\[
D_t = 2\sqrt{\frac{A_t}{\pi}}
\]




\[
L_c = 1.5 D_t
\]




\[
V_c = \pi (D_t/2)^2 L_c
\]



\subsection*{2. Nozzle (Rao)}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
$A_e/A_t$ & 20--40 \\
Angle & 12--18$^\circ$ \\
\end{tabular}



\[
r(z) = r_t + (r_e - r_t)(10t^3 - 15t^4 + 6t^5)
\]





\[
V_n = \int_0^L \pi r(z)^2 dz
\]

\[

\subsection*{4. Turbopump}
